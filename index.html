About
Designing robots from mechanics to interaction.

I am interested in robotic systems where mechanical design, sensing, control, and human interaction are tightly connected.

My current work focuses on robotic manipulation and teleoperation, particularly on interfaces that allow human motion to be transferred to robotic systems in a direct and intuitive manner.

More broadly, I am interested in unconventional robotic mechanisms, wearable devices, soft robotics, and physical intelligence.

Research Interests

Robotic Manipulation

Wearable Robotics

Compact robotic interfaces that physically integrate with the human body and preserve natural interaction.

Novel Mechanisms

Tendon-driven, soft, and unconventional structures that enable new robotic behaviors.
